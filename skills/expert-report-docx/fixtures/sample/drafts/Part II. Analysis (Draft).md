# II. PRACTITIONER FRAMEWORKS AND EMPIRICAL ANALYSIS.

## A. THE GOVERNING FRAMEWORK.

4\. In my experience, securities practitioners look to the underlying governance arrangements rather than labels.[^p2-labels] The governing framework provides:

> Standard practitioner frameworks require disclosure professionals to examine whether a stockholder possesses the practical ability to direct corporate policy through voting power or board composition, whatever label the issuer attaches to that stockholder.

5\. The Roe Report overlooks this literature.[^p2-overlooks] Professor Roe conceded as much at his deposition.[^p2-dep]

6\. For the reasons set forth above, the Roe Report's conclusions do not reflect customary practitioner approaches.[^p2-dep2]
